From bare board to finished fixture

The line runs in sequence, in one facility. Nothing leaves a stage until it passes it.

  1. 01

    PCB mounting

    Solder paste printing and high-speed SMT placement, down to 0402 packages, with per-batch first-article checks.

  2. 02

    Reflow & soldering

    Profiled reflow per board mass, plus selective hand soldering for connectors and through-hole parts.

  3. 03

    PCB inspection

    Automated optical inspection for placement, polarity and joint quality, with a manual review of every flag.

  4. 04

    Driver & electronics

    Drivers matched to the load, then burned in under full current so early failures never reach a site.

  5. 05

    Quality control

    Lumen output, colour temperature, power factor and insulation are measured and logged against the batch.

  6. 06

    Final assembly

    Housing, optics, gasketing and packing — each unit labelled with the board and batch it came from.

An electronics company that happens to make light

Most lighting failures are not optical. A joint cracks, a driver cooks, a capacitor gives up two summers in. AURA LIGHTS was set up to own that part of the problem: we build the board, choose the driver, and test the finished fixture as one system.

That means one team is answerable for the product from paste print to packed carton — and one number to call when a site needs an answer.

  • In-house SMT line, inspection and burn-in
  • Batch traceability from board to finished unit
  • Samples before volume, on every custom build
  • Replacement drivers stocked for supplied projects
